Questions

Before you download

How do I request a part?

Open MTA, add a photo and a short description, choose your car’s make and model, and send. Adding your city is optional.

Who sees my request?

Suppliers who stock parts for your car get notified about it.

How do suppliers get back to me?

They reply in the app chat, where you can send text, photos and voice notes. You can also continue on WhatsApp in one tap.

What if I don’t know the part’s name?

Send a clear photo and say where it sits on the car. Suppliers can ask you questions in chat before they offer anything.

I sell spare parts. Can I join?

Yes. Download MTA and set up your supplier profile. You’ll see requests for the cars you stock and can reply to buyers directly.

Which phones does MTA work on?

iPhone, from the App Store, and Android, from Google Play.
